## Overview

The **Assistive Physical Therapy Powered Table** (developed under the TAYÔ Project as the **LLMo — Lower Limb Mobilization Powered Table**) was created to assist patients and physical therapists across multiple stages of trunk and lower-limb rehabilitation.

Featuring powered support mechanisms for the trunk, upper legs, and lower legs, the platform transitions across **supine, sitting, lift, and standing** configurations while integrating full-body harness support, adjustable transfer height, and tablet-assisted session control.

## From Concept to Patent Grant

Developing a rehabilitation robot required far more than getting a mechanism to move in a laboratory:

- **User Empathy & Clinical Workflow:** Working closely with physical therapists and clinicians reshaped how we approached patient transfers, harness comfort, safety stops, and daily clinic routines across early concepts, Version 1, and Version 2.
- **Cross-Institutional Collaboration:** Engineers, clinicians, academic researchers, IP specialists (DLSU DIPO), and industry value-engineering partners (`P.IMES` and `AUC`) brought distinct risk lenses that surfaced design improvements no single discipline would have spotted alone.
- **Milestone Reality:** A working prototype, safety testing, a granted patent, and commercial adoption are distinct milestones. Earning Philippine Invention Patent **1/2023/050418 B1** (filed 3 August 2023, granted 8 May 2026) represents years of multidisciplinary R&D and iteration.
